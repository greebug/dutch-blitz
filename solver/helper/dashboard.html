Queue: what starts after what

CPU blocks are shares of the whole machine (all threads); GPU blocks are each program's share of GPU time (nvidia-smi pmon), scaled to fit the total. "Cap" is the program's GPU-memory limit. Time left comes from each run's own log (its recent speed); queued runs marked "(guess)" use a rough estimate from helper/dash_notes.json, where the descriptions live too. Updates every few seconds.
